service used by DEEPTRACE is listed here with its source and license. Nothing in this
repository was generated as "AI geometry": the city comes from streamed geographic data,
and infrastructure is drawn as symbolic glyphs unless a licensed model is downloaded.

## 3D models

No third-party 3D models are committed. Infrastructure components are rendered as
symbolic glyphs (billboards and simple instanced primitives), which is a deliberate
visual language rather than an attempt at realistic geometry.

`make assets` (`python -m app.ingest.assets`) downloads **CC0** models from Poly Haven's
public API into `public/assets/models/`, writes `public/assets/models/manifest.json`
(the renderer loads them as close-range level-of-detail models) and appends one row per
model to the section below with its name, source URL and license. Only CC0 assets are
downloaded; Sketchfab or other sources must be added manually after checking that their
license permits the intended use, and recorded here with the required attribution.
